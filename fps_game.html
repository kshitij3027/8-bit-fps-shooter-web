8-Bit FPS Game

Instructions:

  • Click to start the game.
  • W: Move Forward
  • S: Move Backward
  • A: Move Left
  • D: Move Right
  • Click the left mouse button to shoot.
  • Collect power-ups to gain advantages:
    • R: Rapid Fire
    • B: Bullet Speed
    • S: Shield
    • A: Automatic Fire
    • M: Multi-Bullets
    • H: Bullet Honing
    • G: Health Regeneration
    • E: Area-of-Effect Attack
  • Eliminate enemies before they reach you.
